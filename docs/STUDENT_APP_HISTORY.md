# ATTENDANCE Student app 4.5

Student name suggestions: the department's student list (uploaded by the HOD in the staff app) is downloaded from the school server and kept on the phone. On the registration form, typing a name shows matching students; tapping one fills the matric number and level. The saved copy works without internet.

Needs the worker.js from staff app FEG-TECH 4.5 (route /s/roster). If the worker has an ENROL_CODE secret, the list is only downloaded after the student types that code in the "Enrolment code" box.

Camera buttons (4.3): on the live camera, three small buttons at the top right. Switch camera (front/back), Light (the phone torch when the camera has one, otherwise a white screen frame that lights your face), and Improve picture (on by default; brightens the preview, and in a dark room also the picture used for face detection and the registration photo).

Motion (4.4): the app eases in on launch, Register and Home slide between each other, and buttons press in softly, in the iPhone style. Turned off automatically if the phone has Reduce Motion on.

Branding (4.5): Federal University Otuoke crest as the app icon and in the header of every screen, a crest launch screen, and green/gold colours taken from the crest. To refresh the home-screen icon on a phone, remove the app and add it again.
